Why not roll your own?

Rebuilding is about a week of security-sensitive work: WebAuthn parsing, P-256 math, replay-safe CREATE2 accounts, a challenge scheme and a lineage splitter.

The SDK reduces it to a few typed calls — and 云筑家 already exports the scheme JSON.
